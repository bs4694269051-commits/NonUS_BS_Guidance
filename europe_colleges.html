SAT Policy: European universities (Germany, Netherlands, Poland, Ireland, Italy) do not accept SAT — national qualifications (Abitur, Matura, A-Levels, Leaving Cert, VWO) are the sole criteria. French public universities do not accept SAT; Sciences Po Paris is the sole exception (accepted as alternative to entrance exam; competitive applicants score 1350+). Canadian universities do not require SAT; it is accepted as supplementary evidence only — high school transcripts remain primary. Australian & NZ universities do not require SAT; ATAR or IB is primary; a few (Melbourne, Sydney, UNSW) may consider SAT/ACT as an alternative for international students without ATAR.
Always verify directly with each institution before applying.
